# Making Your Research Transparent and Reproducible

This repository contains all materials used for the Making Your Research Transparent and Reproducible class, delivered as part of the SEDarc DTP on 28 January 2026.

This session will guide you step by step in setting up a research process that is transparent and reproducible from start to finish. You will learn what a strong research cycle looks like and how to avoid questionable research practices. We will focus on two key aspects: (1) planning your research, including how and why to pre-register your research plans, and (2) managing your project, covering essential research infrastructure such as backup and version control, code quality and documentation, data archiving, and report writing. The material will be relevant for both quantitative and qualitative research and will include live demonstrations to illustrate best practices. There will be opportunities for questions throughout, followed by a dedicated Q&A session at the end. By the end of the session, you will have the knowledge needed to follow best practices in your own work and understand what should be reported in your thesis.

Most of the class content is based on the slide deck (Making_Your_Res_Transp_and_Repr_slides). The materials used in the live demonstrations (e.g., setting up a project on GitHub or principles for writing high-quality scripts) are provided as R scripts (.R) and R Markdown files (.Rmd).
